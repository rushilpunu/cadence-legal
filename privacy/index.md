# Cadence Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 10 November 2026_

Cadence is a daily journal. What you write is yours, and we never see it.

## What Cadence stores

- Your entries: the sentences you write each day, their Mood Heat, favourites and any photos you attach.
- Your habits and the days you mark them.
- Your settings: Entry Length, Palette, Type Style, reminders, haptics and sound.

These are kept on your device. Entries, habits and photos also sync through **your own private iCloud
database**, so they follow you to your other devices. Apple holds that data under your Apple Account; we can't
read it, and it never reaches a server of ours. We don't run any servers.

## What Cadence does not do

- No account or sign-up.
- No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party code that collects data.
- No sharing or selling of data. We don't have any to share.

## Reminders

Daily reminders are notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

## Purchases

Cadence Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we receive no payment details. The app
asks Apple's StoreKit which plan you have, so it can unlock Pro.

## Deleting your data

Delete any entry from its Day Detail in the app. Deleting the app removes the copy on that device. To remove the
synced copy, open **Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Storage** on iPhone and delete Cadence's data.

## Children

Cadence is not aimed at children and collects no data from anyone.
