# Cadence Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: email [rushilpunukolluyt@gmail.com](mailto:rushilpunukolluyt@gmail.com). I read
everything and usually reply within a couple of days.

## Common questions

**My entries aren't showing on my other device.**
Cadence syncs through your own iCloud. Check both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, that iCloud
Drive is on, and that Cadence is allowed in **Settings → [your name] → iCloud**. Sync can take a few minutes.

**I bought Cadence Pro but it isn't unlocked.**
Open the paywall from the **you** tab and tap **restore purchases**, signed in with the Apple Account you bought
it with.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Open **you → Cadence Pro → manage subscription**, or **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions** on iPhone. You
keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. If you cancel during the 3-day free trial, you aren't charged.

**I missed a day. Is my streak gone?**
Not necessarily. A streak shield covers a single missed day and keeps the streak going.

**How do I delete my data?**
Delete any entry from its day in the app. To remove everything, delete the app, then delete Cadence's data in
**Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Storage**.

## Privacy

Cadence has no accounts, no analytics and no servers. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/).
